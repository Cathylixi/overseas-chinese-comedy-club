ABOUT US

Our Story

The Overseas Chinese Stand-Up Comedy Club was registered in Las Vegas on January 18, 2026. Founder Zhang Xi, a playwright and director, created an original stand-up training program designed for middle-aged overseas Chinese adults.

Through small-group Zoom classes, the club has trained 60 performers from 15 U.S. states. Most members are between 45 and 75 years old and come from highly educated professional communities—including doctors, engineers, software developers, pastors, accountants, entrepreneurs, technology executives, real estate professionals, and retirees.

The club has presented 12 public shows in Las Vegas, Los Angeles, Silicon Valley, and beyond. Its grounded midlife humor and honest immigrant stories have been warmly received in Chinese communities.

FOUNDER

Meet Zhang Xi

Zhang Xi

Playwright / Director / Producer

Zhang Xi is the founder of the North American Maple Culture Center and the Las Vegas Comedy Festival, director of the Overseas Chinese Stand-Up Comedy Club, president of the Chinese American Drama Association, and the first mainland Chinese woman writer-director to present work Off-Broadway. She received formal training in journalism, sociology, and screenwriting, and is currently producing the nonprofit AI film Pearl S. Buck.

Over twelve years, she created twelve original stage plays involving 20 Maple Theatre troupes and 1,800 amateur participants, with 130 performances across 58 cities worldwide. Her work brings overlooked Chinese immigrant histories and contributions to the stage.
